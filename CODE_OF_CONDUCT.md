# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Make space for newcomers and explain project-specific context.
- Protect private information and follow responsible disclosure practices.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and project direction gracefully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith argument.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, secrets, or private repository contents.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional engineering community.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, repetition, and community safety.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile rather than opening a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces, as well as when someone is representing the project publicly.
